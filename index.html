        About TruthLens AI

        TruthLens AI was designed for journalists, researchers, and critical readers who need to quickly inspect whether a claim holds up against reliable evidence.

        How it works

        Rather than relying on a single black-box score, TruthLens combines two distinct signals:

        • Transparent Heuristic Baseline: An in-browser rule set evaluating uppercase overuse, sensational punctuation, clickbait phrasing, emotional language density, source attribution quotes, and concrete dates and numbers. This provides an immediate sanity check.
        • Search-Grounded Gemini Analysis: Server-side reasoning powered by Google Gemini with live Google Search grounding. Gemini extracts key factual claims, cross-references them against verified public reporting, and provides claim-by-claim citations and explanations.

        Copy editor markup

        TruthLens displays evidence directly on the text you paste. Each identified claim is highlighted in place—green for supported, red for contradicted, and amber for unverified—accompanied by aligned margin notes explaining the findings.

        Limitations

        Heuristics can be fooled by well-written satire, and automated AI fact-checking can reflect gaps or delays in available web reporting. In particular, breaking events under active development may not yet have definitive evidence.
